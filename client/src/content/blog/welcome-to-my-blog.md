# Welcome to My Blog

Hello, and welcome to my new blog! This space will be used to share my thoughts on software development, UI/UX design, and progress on my projects.

## Why start a blog?

1. **Documenting the Journey:** As a student at Iowa State University, I am constantly learning new technologies and methods. Writing down what I learn solidifies my understanding.
2. **Sharing Knowledge:** Helping others who might be facing the same bugs or architectural dilemmas.
3. **Writing Practice:** Clear writing is a superpower in software engineering, especially for technical specifications, documentation, and team alignment.
