# Welcome to My Blog

Hello, and welcome to my new blog! This space will be used to share my thoughts on software development, learning experiences, and thoughts about things going on in tech.

## Why start a blog?

1. **Documenting the Journey:** As a student at Iowa State University, I am constantly learning new technologies and methods. Writing down what I learn solidifies my understanding. It also allows me to have something to look back on.
2. **Sharing Knowledge:** Helping others who might be facing the same bugs or architectural dilemmas.
3. **Writing Practice:** Clear writing is a superpower in software engineering, especially for technical specifications, documentation, and team alignment.
4. **Its fun**: Writing out my thoughts freely is something that I find to be enjoyable. I also use a physical journal to document my life. The blog will be there to document my career.
5. **Better than LinkedIn**: This allows me to showcase myself more casually than the polished up tight version of myself on LinkedIn. I can also write lengthier pieces that would appeal to anyone who is digging into me. 

## How often will I post? 

As often as I feel, this blog is mainly a hobby space for me to put my thoughts out into the void. This being said, given that I am a student with a job and other interests, there will likely be large stretches of zero activity.

## Being realistic

I am fairly certain that not a soul besides myself will be reading any of these blogs. That being said, people get famous on accident, so I will not be writing about things that are too personal or sensitive. 

All blogs will be completely AI free, just my own brain typing on the computer. For transparency, I did use AI to create this website, and I use AI to polish up the detailed posts about my projects. Everything in those posts is validated by my eyeballs, so no need to worry about that. AI is just a much better writer than I am for polished professional prose. 

The effort level put into these blogs will appear to be much less than my project posts. Which, is the truth. The blog section is a space for me to put thoughts to page, whether or not they even make sense, they will be genuine. 

## Conclusion

Welcome again! Feel free to reach out to me. Happy reading!
