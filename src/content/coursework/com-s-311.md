# COM S 311: Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms

An in-depth study of the design and analysis of algorithms. This course covers:

- **Algorithm Analysis:** Growth of functions, asymptotic notation, and solving recurrences.
- **Design Paradigms:** Greedy algorithms, Divide and Conquer, and Dynamic Programming.
- **Graph Algorithms:** Breadth-first search, depth-first search, topological sort, minimum spanning trees (Kruskal's and Prim's), and shortest paths (Dijkstra's, Bellman-Ford).
- **NP-Completeness:** Understand class P and NP, polynomial-time reductions, and NP-complete problems.
